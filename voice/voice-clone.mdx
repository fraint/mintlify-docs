Voice Clone replicates a vocal tone from your uploaded audio samples to create a personalized custom voice. Use it when you want the synthetic voice to match a specific real-world voice.

## Create a voice clone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create My Voice">
    Open the Volink dashboard and click **Create My Voice** to begin creating a custom voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Voice Clone">
    Select **Voice Clone** from the list of voice creation options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a name and select a voice model">
    Enter a **name** for your cloned voice, then select the **voice model** you want to use. Available voice models include MiniMax Speech 02 Turbo, ByteDance Open Speech TTS v3, SenseTime Daily Innovation TTS v1, and CosyVoice2-0.5B.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload an audio file">
    Upload an **audio file** containing your recording sample. This recording is the source Volink uses to replicate the vocal tone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select language and gender">
    Choose the **language** and **gender** that match the voice in your recording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter preview text">
    Enter **preview text** to hear how the cloned voice sounds before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save without preview">
    Click **Save without preview** to finalize the voice clone and make it available for use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Only clone voices you have the right to use. Do not clone another person's voice without their explicit consent.
</Warning>

## Audio sample requirements

The quality of the cloned voice depends directly on the quality of your recording. Follow these guidelines for best results:

- **Clear recording** — Use a recording with consistent volume and minimal distortion. Muffled or clipped audio reduces clone accuracy.
- **Minimal background noise** — Record in a quiet environment. Ambient noise, echo, or music interferes with the cloning process.
- **Representative of the target voice** — The sample should capture the natural speaking style, pace, and tone of the voice you want to replicate. Avoid atypical delivery such as whispering, shouting, or exaggerated emotion unless those qualities are intentional.
