Voice Mix combines multiple voice characters to produce a custom conversational effect. Use it when you want a blended voice identity that draws from multiple voice personalities.

## Create a voice mix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create My Voice">
    Open the Volink dashboard and click **Create My Voice** to begin creating a custom voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Voice Mix">
    Select **Voice Mix** from the list of voice creation options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a name and select a voice model">
    Enter a **name** for your voice mix, then select the **voice model** you want to use as the foundation. Available voice models include MiniMax Speech 02 Turbo, ByteDance Open Speech TTS v3, SenseTime Daily Innovation TTS v1, and CosyVoice2-0.5B.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Voice">
    Click **Add Voice** to begin adding individual voice characters to your mix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select language and gender">
    Choose the **language** and **gender** for the voice character you are adding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter preview text">
    Enter **preview text** to hear how your voice mix sounds before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Click **Save** to finalize and store your voice mix.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Preview your mix with a representative phrase before saving to ensure it matches your use case. A phrase typical of your actual content gives you the most accurate preview.
</Tip>

## Advanced settings

Go to **Settings** on the voice mix page to adjust precise parameters for higher customization. Use these controls to fine-tune the blend of voices and shape the final conversational effect to your exact requirements.
