Text-Designed Voice lets you describe a voice using natural language, and Volink generates a matching custom voice. This is the fastest way to create a unique voice without audio samples.

## Create a text-designed voice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create My Voice">
    Open the Volink dashboard and click **Create My Voice** to begin creating a custom voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Text-Designed Voice">
    Select **Text-Designed Voice** from the list of voice creation options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a name and select a voice model">
    Enter a **name** for your voice, then select the **voice model** you want to use. Available voice models include MiniMax Speech 02 Turbo, ByteDance Open Speech TTS v3, SenseTime Daily Innovation TTS v1, and CosyVoice2-0.5B.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a prompt describing the voice">
    Enter a **prompt describing the voice** you want to generate. For example: "calm, professional, mid-pitched female voice." The more specific your description, the more accurately the generated voice will match your intent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter preview text">
    Enter **preview text** to hear how the generated voice sounds before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save without preview">
    Click **Save without preview** to finalize your voice and make it available for use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Be specific in your prompt. Instead of "friendly voice," try "warm, upbeat, medium-paced female voice with a slight American accent." Precise descriptions produce more predictable and useful results.
</Tip>

## Writing effective prompts

A well-written prompt gives the voice generator clear, actionable characteristics to work with. Include as many of the following attributes as are relevant to your use case:

- **Tone** — Describe the overall emotional quality, such as calm, energetic, authoritative, or warm.
- **Pitch** — Specify high, mid, or low pitch to set the vocal register.
- **Pace** — Indicate how quickly or slowly the voice should speak, such as slow, medium-paced, or brisk.
- **Gender** — State the intended gender to guide the voice character.
- **Accent** — Mention a regional or national accent if consistency is important for your audience.
- **Emotion** — Add emotional nuance such as cheerful, serious, empathetic, or neutral.

Combining multiple attributes in a single prompt yields more consistent results. For example: "low-pitched, slow-paced, authoritative male voice with a neutral British accent" gives the generator a complete picture rather than leaving characteristics to chance.
