---
title: "Points & billing"
description: "Understand how Volink's points system works, how to check your consumption, purchase more points, and which payment methods are accepted."
---

Volink uses a points-based billing system. You purchase a bundle of points in advance, and those points are consumed each time you make an API call. The number of points deducted per call depends on the model you use and the volume of input and output in that request.

## Check your points usage

- Open the **Dashboard** to view **Cost**, **Requests**, **Tokens**, and **Latency** for today and the last 30 days.
- Open **Logs** to see per-request details and identify which calls are using the most points.

<Tip>
  Monitor your usage regularly. Checking the dashboard metrics before a project launch or high-traffic period helps you avoid unexpected quota depletion mid-deployment.
</Tip>

## Purchase points

When your points balance runs low, top up directly from your dashboard:

1. Click **Top up** in the **Balance** block in the lower-left corner.
2. Select the plan that fits your usage needs.

## Payment methods

Volink accepts the following payment methods when purchasing a points plan:

- **Credit Card**
- **Alipay**

## Understanding consumption

Points are consumed per API call. The exact amount depends on the model you select and the size of your request — longer inputs and outputs use more points. Review aggregate consumption on the **Dashboard** and per-request details in **Logs** to identify which requests account for the most spend.
